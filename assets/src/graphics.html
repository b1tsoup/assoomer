Agent
"More detailed" could go a lot of ways, and most of them mean writing product claims only you can decide.
  1. Which sections should change: Hero, Features, Pricing, or new ones?
  2. What should the new text say? I won't make up product claims or testimonials.
  3. Can the layout change, or only the content?
Once you answer, it builds the page with your content, not its guesses.
Things the agent made up without Assoomer
1 What the product does: "plan projects, share files"
2 A pricing promise: "free forever, no credit card required"
3 A security claim: "encrypted in transit and at rest"
4 Plan limits and features for Free and Pro
5 FAQ questions, like "How is my data protected?"
Design it assumed: the usual AI landing-page template
A Centered hero with two buttons
B "How it works" in 3 numbered steps
C Dark "Ready to bring your team together?" banner at the bottom
It looks polished, but none of it was decided by you. Because the agent guessed, it went with the most generic version.

"Realistic content" means writing quotes, names and companies that would be published as statements from customers. I can't make those up.
  1. Can you paste 3 real testimonials? If not, should I use clearly marked placeholders?
  2. Avatars: your images, initials, or a placeholder service?
  3. Match the current plain style, or a new one?
  4. Hover effect: lift, blue border, or both?
